# Personas

## Maya — Senior software engineer (primary)

Maya owns checkout reliability. She needs to resolve a serious bug quickly, but cannot trust an agent that gives an unexplained patch. She wants a reproducible test, visible evidence, and a pull request she can review.

## Leo — Engineering lead (secondary)

Leo needs the team’s reasoning to survive handoffs. He wants decisions, incidents, and verified behavior to remain searchable and auditable rather than being lost in chat history.

## Priya — Platform / AI engineering lead (future buyer)

Priya is rolling out coding agents. Her core concern is not generation quality alone; it is whether agents can be evaluated, constrained, and improved over time.
